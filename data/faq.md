# Spice Garden – Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you serve alcohol?
No. Spice Garden is a non-alcoholic restaurant. We offer mocktails, lassis, fresh juices and traditional drinks instead.

## Is the restaurant suitable for children?
Yes. We have high chairs available, and our chefs can prepare mild, less spicy versions of dishes for kids on request.

## Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible?
Yes. The restaurant has a ramp at the entrance, a lift from the ground floor, and an accessible washroom.

## Do you host birthday parties and events?
Yes. Our garden area can be booked for private parties of 15 to 60 guests. Please call +91 80 4567 8900 at least 3 days in advance to arrange a party menu.

## Is Wi-Fi available?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for all dine-in guests. Please ask the staff for the password.

## Do you offer a buffet?
We offer a weekday lunch buffet (Monday to Friday, 12:00 PM – 3:30 PM) at ₹499 per person, with 2 starters, 4 mains, breads, rice and 2 desserts. There is no buffet at dinner or on weekends.

## Are there any offers or discounts?
- 10% off on the total bill for students with a valid college ID on weekdays.
- Complimentary dessert for guests celebrating a birthday (valid ID required).
- Offers cannot be combined with each other.

## Which dishes are your bestsellers?
Our most popular dishes are Butter Chicken, Paneer Butter Masala, Dal Makhani, Chicken Dum Biryani and Gulab Jamun.

## Is the food halal?
Yes. All chicken and mutton served at Spice Garden is sourced from halal-certified suppliers.

## Can I order food for delivery through this assistant?
The assistant cannot place new food orders. Please order through our phone line or popular food delivery apps. The assistant can track an existing order if you share your order ID.

## Do you have gift cards?
Yes. Gift cards worth ₹500, ₹1,000 and ₹2,000 can be purchased at the front desk and are valid for 6 months.
