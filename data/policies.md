# Spice Garden – Restaurant Information & Policies

## About Spice Garden
Spice Garden is a family-friendly multi-cuisine Indian restaurant serving North Indian, South Indian and coastal dishes since 2012. We seat up to 80 guests across an indoor air-conditioned hall and an outdoor garden area.

## Opening Hours
- Monday to Friday: 11:00 AM until 12:00 AM (midnight)
- Saturday and Sunday: 11:00 AM until 2:00 AM the following calendar day
- Weekend overnight hours continue after midnight: Saturday service runs until 2:00 AM Sunday, and Sunday service runs until 2:00 AM Monday. Friday service closes at midnight, so Friday-to-Saturday after-midnight hours are closed.
- The kitchen takes last orders 30 minutes before closing.
- We are closed on Diwali and Holi. On other public holidays we follow weekend timings.

## Parking
Free valet parking is available for dine-in guests from 7:00 PM onwards. During lunch, paid parking is available at City Mall across the road.

## Table Reservation Policy
- Reservations can be made through this chat assistant, by phone, or at the front desk.
- Reservations are accepted for parties of 1 to 10 guests. For groups larger than 10, please call us for a group or party booking.
- Reservations can be made up to 30 days in advance.
- Reservation slots are every 30 minutes during opening hours. The final slot is 30 minutes before closing. Early-morning reservation times belong to the previous day's overnight session when that session is open.
- We hold a reserved table for 15 minutes past the booking time. After that the table may be released to walk-in guests.
- Walk-ins are welcome, subject to availability.

## Cancellation Policy
- Reservations can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 2 hours before the booking time by calling us.
- Repeated no-shows (3 or more) may lead to future reservations requiring confirmation by phone.
- Group bookings of more than 10 guests require a refundable advance of ₹1,000, which is refunded if cancelled at least 24 hours in advance.

## Delivery and Takeaway
- We deliver within a 7 km radius of the restaurant.
- Minimum order value for delivery is ₹300. Delivery is free above ₹800, otherwise a ₹40 delivery fee applies.
- Typical delivery time is 35–50 minutes.
- Takeaway orders can be placed by phone and are usually ready in 20–25 minutes.
- Every order gets an order ID (for example ORD1001), which can be used to track the order through this assistant.

## Payment Methods
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and meal cards (Sodexo/Pluxee). A 5% GST is applied to the bill. We do not add a service charge; tipping is optional.

## Food, Allergy and Dietary Policy
- All dishes are marked vegetarian or non-vegetarian. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian food is cooked in separate sections of the kitchen.
- Several dishes are vegan: Hara Bhara Kebab, Chana Masala, Tandoori Roti, Jeera Rice, Mango Sorbet and Fresh Lime Soda.
- Most dishes can be made less spicy or extra spicy on request.
- Our kitchen handles nuts, dairy, gluten, soy and fish. While we take care, we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free of allergens. Guests with severe allergies should inform the staff before ordering.
- We do not use MSG in any dish.
- Jain food (no onion, no garlic) can be prepared for most vegetarian mains on request.

## Dress Code and House Rules
- Smart casual. There is no strict dress code.
- Smoking is not allowed inside the restaurant. A designated smoking zone is available near the parking area.
- Outside food and beverages are not allowed, except birthday cakes (a ₹200 cake-cutting fee applies).
- Pets are allowed in the outdoor garden seating only.
